Counting methods

Publications are deduplicated by PMID, DOI, or normalized reference text within each modality. One paper testing several effectors counts once in the annual publication chart and once for each effector in its own publication count. Use a consistent PMID or DOI across records of the same paper. A paper cited only by DOI in one row and PMID in another requires curator reconciliation.

Effector timelines use stable Atlas IDs and the earliest dated qualifying report in this dataset. Different constructs do not create additional effectors. Reports need a reference, a publication year, and status “yes” to enter the time curves. Missing years are not inferred. Conflicting years for one reference use the earliest entered year and should be reviewed. The last-three-years window includes the release year and its two preceding calendar years.

PROTAC references are selectively curated; their counts should not be interpreted as complete field coverage or directly compared with bioPROTAC coverage. Counts include different effector classes, including E2s. Notes and limitations remain attached to individual reports.

About & acknowledgment

A resource for bioPROTAC design

The TPD Effector Atlas organizes published applications of effectors in targeted protein degradation, with an emphasis on bioPROTACs and genetically encoded approaches. It connects effector identities, reported targets, constructs and experimental notes to original publications.

Developed and maintained by Shang Su and the Su Laboratory, Department of Comparative Biomedical Sciences, School of Veterinary Medicine, Louisiana State University.

Coverage is evolving. Reported applications are evidence of use in a defined system, not a ranking of effectiveness. PROTAC coverage includes representative references and reported targets; visit PROTAC-DB for detailed chemical information.

Classification follows recruitment mechanism: PROTAC uses a heterobifunctional binder to bridge effector and target; bioPROTAC directly fuses the effector to a target binder; MGD induces or stabilizes a degradation-associated interaction. Genetic encoding alone does not determine the category.
